// The Problem

FADED LINES COST YOU MORE THAN YOU THINK

Every season, GTA property owners pay the price for neglected pavement markings — in fines, liability, and lost trust from tenants and customers.

⚖️
Municipal Fines
Faded handicap symbols and fire lanes are bylaw violations across every GTA municipality. The fine costs far more than the repaint.
🛡️
Insurance & Liability
If someone is injured in your lot and markings are faded, your liability exposure increases significantly. Visible lines are your first line of protection.
🌨️
Ontario Winters
GTA winters destroy pavement markings every single year. Spring restriping isn't optional — it's annual maintenance, like any other property upkeep.
👁️
First Impressions
Your parking lot is the first thing every customer sees. A faded, disorganized lot signals that nothing inside is well-maintained either.
